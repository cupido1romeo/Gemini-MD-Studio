## 2025-05-15 - Improving Toolbar Accessibility and Interaction
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons in toolbars are a common accessibility gap. Adding ARIA labels, focus-visible rings, and proper ARIA menu roles significantly improves the experience for screen reader and keyboard users without affecting the visual design for mouse users.
**Action:** Always verify icon-only buttons for aria-labels and use focus-visible:ring-2 for consistent, accessible focus states.
